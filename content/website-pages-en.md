## International Certification & Management Systems
URL: `/international-certification/`
Description: Implementation and readiness support for management systems and international certification schemes.

We support organisations introducing management systems and preparing for independent assessment against recognised international standards. Our work focuses on readiness, implementation quality, internal verification and practical integration into operations.
### Standards and schemes we can support- ISO 9001 for quality management.
- ISO 14001 for environmental management.
- ISO 45001 for occupational health and safety.
- ISO 22000 and FSSC 22000 for food safety management.
- ISO 27001, ISO 37001 and other management-system standards where relevant to the client.
- Sector-specific schemes such as GLOBALG.A.P., organic requirements and selected industry standards, depending on scope.

### What we do- Gap assessments against standard requirements and customer expectations.
- Implementation planning, procedure development and documentation support.
- Training for management teams, internal auditors and operational personnel.
- Internal audits, management review preparation and corrective-action follow-up.
- Coordination support for readiness before certification-body assessment.

### Important noteAGRO FOOD CHAIN provides consulting, training and independent second-party support. Accredited certificates are issued only by authorised certification bodies under their own rules and decision-making processes.

## Food Safety & Quality Management
URL: `/food-safety/`
Description: HACCP, ISO 22000, FSSC 22000 and practical food-safety system support for processors and supply chains.

We support food manufacturers, exporters, warehouses and related operations in building and strengthening food-safety systems that meet international requirements and customer expectations.
### Key standards and topics- HACCP and hazard-based food-safety planning.
- ISO 22000:2018 food safety management systems.
- FSSC 22000 including prerequisite programmes and additional requirements.
- BRCGS and customer food-safety expectations where relevant to the operation.
- Traceability, supplier approval, food defence, food fraud mitigation and allergen management.
- Cleaning and sanitation, environmental monitoring, validation and verification activities.

### What we do- Conduct gap assessments and technical reviews of the current food-safety system.
- Develop or improve HACCP studies, flow diagrams, PRP programmes and supporting procedures.
- Support implementation of traceability, nonconformity handling, corrective action and verification systems.
- Train teams on hygiene, CCP/OPRP monitoring, internal audits and food-safety culture.
- Carry out internal audits and readiness checks before customer or certification-body visits.

### Typical deliverables- Gap-assessment report and action plan.
- Documented HACCP package and core procedures.
- Internal-audit reports, verification records and improvement recommendations.
- Training materials and implementation guidance tailored to the facility.

## Social Compliance & Ethical Sourcing
URL: `/social-compliance/`
Description: Supplier labour, workplace and responsible-sourcing assessments with practical follow-up support.

We help buyers and suppliers understand labour, human-rights and workplace risks through evidence-based reviews and practical improvement planning.
### Typical reference frameworks- Sedex / SMETA expectations and related customer social-compliance requirements.
- amfori BSCI expectations and responsible sourcing requirements.
- Buyer codes of conduct and labour-rights expectations.
- Occupational health and safety, grievance, recruitment and working-condition requirements.

### What we do- Review employment documentation, working hours, wages, age controls and management practices.
- Assess health and safety controls, welfare conditions and worker communication channels.
- Conduct ethical worker interviews and site observations, subject to scope and consent arrangements.
- Identify nonconformities, risks and corrective-action priorities.
- Support suppliers in preparing improvement plans and follow-up verification.

### Important noteA locally conducted second-party assessment is not automatically an official SMETA or amfori BSCI audit. Formal programme-specific audits are performed only under the applicable programme rules and approved auditor arrangements.

#### Need a responsible-sourcing assessment?We can structure a supplier review focused on labour conditions, worker welfare and practical corrective actions.[/contact/ -> Discuss a social-compliance assessment →]
