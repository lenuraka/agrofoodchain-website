# AGRO FOOD CHAIN Expert Contributed to UNCTAD Training on GLOBALG.A.P. and Dried Fruit Quality

**Category:** International Cooperation / Project Archive  
**Event:** 25–26 May 2021, online / Uzbekistan  
**Prepared for website:** 21 November 2026  
**URL slug:** unctad-globalgap-dried-fruit-uzbekistan-2021

Archive: In May 2021, AGRO FOOD CHAIN specialist Lenur Eredjepov delivered GLOBALG.A.P. training sessions in a UNCTAD programme for Uzbekistan's dried-fruit value chain.

## Expert contribution: GLOBALG.A.P. requirements

The UNCTAD event page names **Lenur Eredjepov, Inspector-Consultant at AgroFoodChain LLC**, as presenter of two sessions entitled “Global GAP – International Standard Requirements on Fruits and Vegetable Production (I & II).”

The national training took place online on **25–26 May 2021**, providing public- and private-sector stakeholders with information on international quality standards and certification systems for Uzbekistan’s dried-fruit value chain.

The GLOBALG.A.P. sessions addressed farm-level, crop-level and fruit-and-vegetable production requirements, including inputs, harvesting, storage and post-harvest handling.

## A wider focus on export quality

The training formed part of UNCTAD’s initiative to help landlocked commodity-dependent countries participate more effectively in regional and global value chains. The programme also covered organic production requirements, drying technologies, UNECE standards for dried grapes and dried-fruit food-safety issues, with other specialists delivering those sessions.
