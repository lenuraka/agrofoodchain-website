ESG & Sustainability

We support organisations that need to strengthen environmental, social and governance practices in response to customer, investor or project requirements.

Reference areas

  • ESG screening and risk assessment based on agreed client criteria.
  • Environmental management topics including ISO 14001 principles.
  • Occupational health and safety topics linked to ISO 45001 principles.
  • Resource efficiency, water stewardship, waste management and climate-related action planning.
  • Supply-chain sustainability, corrective-action tracking and stakeholder engagement.

What we do

  • Conduct site-level ESG reviews and supplier sustainability assessments.
  • Support action plans for environmental and social improvements.
  • Help structure policies, responsibilities, monitoring indicators and reporting logic.
  • Contribute to supplier-improvement programmes and sustainability capacity building.
  • Follow up implementation progress through agreed verification activities.

Who we support

  • Manufacturers and exporters working with international buyers.
  • Financial institutions and investors requiring site-level information.
  • Development projects and programmes supporting sustainable supply chains.

Need ESG implementation support?

We can help define the assessment scope, practical actions and follow-up approach for your site, supplier or project.
