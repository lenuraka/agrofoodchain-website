AGRO FOOD CHAIN Expert Contributed to UNCTAD Training on GLOBALG.A.P. and Dried Fruit Quality

INTERNATIONAL COOPERATION · PROJECT ARCHIVE · 2021

Project archive · 25–26 May 2021 · Uzbekistan. AGRO FOOD CHAIN LLC contributed specialist expertise to a national training organised by the United Nations Conference on Trade and Development (UNCTAD) on international certification, standards and quality throughout Uzbekistan’s dried-fruit value chain.

Expert contribution: GLOBALG.A.P. requirements

The UNCTAD event page names Lenur Eredjepov, Inspector-Consultant at AgroFoodChain LLC, as the presenter of two sessions, Global GAP – International Standard Requirements on Fruits and Vegetable Production (I & II). The training took place on 25–26 May 2021 in an online format for stakeholders in Uzbekistan’s dried-fruit sector.

The official training programme focused on good agricultural practices at farm, crop, and fruit-and-vegetable production levels, including inputs, harvesting, storage and post-harvest handling. These requirements matter for farm compliance, traceability and meeting the expectations of export markets.

A wider focus on export quality

Organised in the context of UNCTAD’s project on integrating landlocked commodity-dependent developing countries into regional and global value chains, the event also addressed organic certification, drying technologies, UNECE quality standards for dried grapes and food-safety hazards in dried-fruit production. These other sessions were delivered by the respective specialists listed in the official programme.

For agricultural producers, processors and exporters, consistent quality controls from field to finished product can strengthen market readiness and support more reliable international trade.
