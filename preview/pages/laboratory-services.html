Laboratory Services & Accreditation

We support laboratories and organisations that rely on laboratory testing in strengthening management systems, competence and readiness for accreditation or customer requirements.

Key standards and topics

  • ISO/IEC 17025 requirements for testing and calibration laboratories.
  • Method control, validation or verification, sampling and uncertainty topics where relevant.
  • Equipment management, calibration, maintenance and test-environment controls.
  • Record control, nonconforming work, internal audits and corrective action.
  • Interlaboratory comparisons, proficiency-testing coordination and laboratory readiness activities.

What we do

  • Conduct gap reviews of laboratory management systems and technical controls.
  • Support documentation, process mapping and implementation planning.
  • Review testing-workflow controls, sample handling and result-reporting practices.
  • Provide training and readiness checks before external assessment.
  • Support organisations in structuring laboratory-related supplier or project requirements.

Strengthening laboratory capability?

We can help clarify the roadmap for ISO/IEC 17025 readiness and practical system improvement.
