Food Safety & Quality Management

We support food manufacturers, exporters, warehouses and related operations in building and strengthening food-safety systems that meet international requirements and customer expectations.

Key standards and topics

  • HACCP and hazard-based food-safety planning.
  • ISO 22000:2018 food safety management systems.
  • FSSC 22000 including prerequisite programmes and additional requirements.
  • BRCGS and customer food-safety expectations where relevant to the operation.
  • Traceability, supplier approval, food defence, food fraud mitigation and allergen management.
  • Cleaning and sanitation, environmental monitoring, validation and verification activities.

What we do

  • Conduct gap assessments and technical reviews of the current food-safety system.
  • Develop or improve HACCP studies, flow diagrams, PRP programmes and supporting procedures.
  • Support implementation of traceability, nonconformity handling, corrective action and verification systems.
  • Train teams on hygiene, CCP/OPRP monitoring, internal audits and food-safety culture.
  • Carry out internal audits and readiness checks before customer or certification-body visits.

Typical deliverables

  • Gap-assessment report and action plan.
  • Documented HACCP package and core procedures.
  • Internal-audit reports, verification records and improvement recommendations.
  • Training materials and implementation guidance tailored to the facility.
