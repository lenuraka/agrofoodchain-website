Agricultural Standards & Sustainable Production

We assist farms, greenhouses, producer groups and agri-food exporters in improving agricultural practices, documentation and readiness for internationally recognised farm and sustainability requirements.

Standards and frameworks

  • GLOBALG.A.P. IFA and related farm-management requirements.
  • GRASP and selected social-responsibility expectations at farm level.
  • EU Organic requirements and USDA NOP readiness, where relevant.
  • Good Agricultural Practice, harvest hygiene, input control and traceability expectations.
  • Water quality, pesticide management, fertiliser use, worker hygiene and field records.

What we do

  • Review farm practices, records, maps and operational controls against applicable requirements.
  • Support farm documentation, record-keeping, traceability and internal control improvements.
  • Assess irrigation, pesticide-use records, harvest handling and product-identification practices.
  • Train managers and field teams on compliance, hygiene, worker practices and record completion.
  • Prepare farms for customer visits, certification audits or improvement programmes.

Typical clients

  • Fruit and vegetable farms, greenhouses and orchards.
  • Producer groups, exporters and packhouses.
  • Development programmes promoting good agricultural practice and sustainable production.

Working with farms or exporters?

We can help align field operations and records with GLOBALG.A.P., organic and buyer requirements.
