International Development Projects

AGRO FOOD CHAIN works with development partners, programmes and institutions that need regional support in Central Asia for standards-based initiatives, capacity building and field implementation.

Typical project support areas

  • Good Agricultural Practice and sustainable production programmes.
  • Food safety, market-access and value-chain strengthening initiatives.
  • Supplier and beneficiary assessments, baseline reviews and field verification.
  • Training, coaching, awareness-raising and local stakeholder engagement.
  • Monitoring, follow-up and practical implementation support.

What we do

  • Support project design with technical input and field realities from the region.
  • Conduct assessments, site visits and implementation follow-up in Central Asia.
  • Develop practical tools, guidance and training content for beneficiaries.
  • Facilitate corrective-action follow-up and local coordination where required.
  • Provide clear reporting against the agreed project scope and deliverables.

Who we work with

  • International development organisations and donor-funded programmes.
  • Banks, projects and implementing partners supporting agribusiness and supply-chain improvement.
  • Private-sector programmes with a capacity-building or regional implementation component.
