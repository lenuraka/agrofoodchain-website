Supplier Audits & Due Diligence

International buyers and investors often need reliable, local insight before approving suppliers or expanding sourcing. We support this need with independent, scope-defined supplier assessments and due diligence activities.

What we assess

  • Factory capability, process controls and management-system maturity.
  • Food safety, hygiene, traceability and operational controls where relevant.
  • Labour conditions, documentation and workplace practices.
  • Environmental, health and safety and compliance-related operational risks.
  • Supplier corrective-action progress and ongoing monitoring needs.

What we do

  • Agree the audit protocol, reporting format, confidentiality approach and project scope.
  • Perform document review and on-site verification activities.
  • Collect factual evidence and summarise findings in a structured report.
  • Support classification of issues, risk ratings and practical corrective-action planning where requested.
  • Conduct follow-up verification of supplier corrective actions, subject to agreement.

Typical clients

  • International buyers, importers, retailers and sourcing teams.
  • Banks, investors and organisations requiring independent supplier verification.
  • Development programmes or projects screening supplier readiness.
