International Buyers

For international procurement teams, supplier verification across borders can be difficult. We support buyers with local execution and structured reporting.

  • Factory capability reviews and product-process understanding.
  • Food safety, social compliance or ESG-focused audits depending on the sourcing objective.
  • Corrective-action follow-up and progress verification.
  • Regional coordination with clear, factual reporting.
