About Us

AGRO FOOD CHAIN LLC is based in Uzbekistan and works in international standards consulting, auditing and certification-support activities for organisations operating in Central Asia or sourcing from the region.

Our approach

  • Practical, evidence-based work aligned to the agreed scope.
  • Understanding of regional business realities and international expectations.
  • Clear distinction between consulting support, independent assessments and accredited certification decisions.
  • Commitment to structured reporting and implementable corrective actions.

Who we work with

  • Manufacturers, agribusinesses, farms and exporters.
  • International buyers and sourcing teams.
  • Financial institutions, investors and development partners.
  • Laboratories and organisations strengthening management systems.

AGRO FOOD CHAIN is a representative and partner of RINA in Uzbekistan. Specific services, approvals and contractual responsibilities are defined per engagement.
